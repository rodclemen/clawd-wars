# What's new in Clawd Wars

The changes you'd notice in the Star Claws universe, newest first. The full, detailed history (every fix, every version) lives in [Clawd Clash](https://github.com/rodclemen/clawd-clash).

## 3.0.0: Clawd Wars joins Clawd Clash

- **One app, three universes.** Clawd Wars is now part of Clawd Clash. Star Claws keeps the whole cast (81 characters) and all 22 places, and you can switch to **Clawvel** or **Detective Claws** in Settings. Switching recasts everyone on screen and moves the scene to the new universe.
- **Parody names.** Star Wars became **Star Claws**, and every character and place has a new name: Darth Vapor, Luke Skyclawer, Han Yolo, Princess Laya, the Millennial Falcon, the Mos Easy Cantina and the rest. The swords are laser swords now, and the lightning is dark lightning.
- **Random universe.** Pick **Random** and the app changes universe on its own every 1 to 60 minutes, with a wipe across the whole window.
- **A Settings window.** Every option in one place (universe, window, desktop, connected AIs, screen saver), and a much shorter menu.
- **The window steps aside** when no AI is running, and comes back when one starts.
- **Keep Characters on Top** can be switched off, so the desktop crew lives behind your windows instead of over them.
- **Everyone looks the same as before.** The Star Claws crew was carried over frame by frame, and Sebulbous now fires a flame jet and the Clawa a blue ion bolt instead of the usual red.
- **The screen saver picks its own universe** in its Options, and a few screen saver glitches were fixed (it no longer disappears from System Settings' list).
- **Upgrading takes care of itself.** Clawd Clash takes over the AIs the old app had connected, quits the old app and starts in Star Claws, keeping your sessions' characters.
- A new icon: a comic cover with one panel per universe.

## 2.26 to 2.28: the screen saver and a bigger cast

- **A screen saver**: the place at full screen, the crew fighting across its floor, a card per session, name tags, a clock and a crawl of what happened lately. It wipes somewhere new every 10 minutes, and puts on a show of its own when nothing much is running.
- It shows your sessions **even with the app closed**.
- **The cast grew from 30 to 81**, with characters who only ever turn up as agents.
- **Han Yolo shoots first.** Han Yolo and Greedy-O now face off whenever both are on stage, and every so often Greedy-O hits the floor.
- **Idle habits and signature moves**: idle characters stop fighting and fidget, spin their swords, blow the smoke off their blasters, or do something all their own.
- "Needs you" keeps asking until you answer.
- The window runs the place right up to the top, with no title bar in the way.
- A monochrome menu bar icon, if you prefer one.

## 2.18 to 2.25: more AIs, and a better window

- **Codex, Qwen Code, Gemini CLI and GitHub Copilot CLI** join Claude Code. With two AIs running they take opposite sides: light side against dark side.
- Each AI has its own outline colour, and sessions in the Claude desktop app are told apart from the terminal ones.
- **Projects are named after their repo**, with a little pill saying where it lives (GitHub, GitLab or local).
- Sessions in the same project are grouped together, and long agent lists fold away.
- **Clawd Wars opens in a window by default**, at a compact size.
- **Double-click the menu bar icon** to hide or show the crew.
- Wave off an AI you don't use, and it stops asking to connect it.
- Many places redrawn from the films they come from, and blaster shoot-outs gained ducks, shields and bolts colliding mid-air.
- Much lighter on your Mac: about a fifth of the CPU it used before.

## 2.0 to 2.17: window mode and 22 places

- **Window mode**: the crew in a scene, with a card for every session showing what it's doing, your last prompt and its agents.
- **22 places**, each with a little animation, from the Mos Easy Cantina to the Battle of Froth. The scene goes where the first session's character belongs, or you pick one.
- **The Millennial Falcon picks Han Yolo up** when his agent is done.
- New characters, including the Rancor, Greedy-O, a Snowtaunt, the Chicken Walker, the AT-ACK and Wicker the Furwok.
- **Hover** any character for the details; newcomers walk in from the side.
- **Homebrew install**, and an offer to connect your AIs on first launch.
- Agents show what they're doing in a label over their heads.
- The name became **Clawd Wars**, with its own icon.

## 1.x: where it started

- A single Claude in a dark helmet on your desktop, swinging a sword while it works, with a thought bubble, a red `!` when it needs you, and agents joining as rebels.
- Then several Claudes at once, each with its own villain, fighting each other: sword duels, deflected blaster bolts, dark lightning.
- Click a character to jump to its terminal or editor tab.
- The Playground, to put anyone on stage and watch them fight.
